# The Whispering Waves

**Logline:** A retired lighthouse keeper uncovers the secrets of the past through an old radio that picks up mysterious messages from vanished ships.

**Theme:** connection to the past  
**Tone:** mysterious  
**Target Duration:** 150s

## Characters

- **Jack Harris** (retired lighthouse keeper): A solitary, aged man with a deep sense of nostalgia and longing for the past.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Discovery
- **Setting:** lighthouse attic
- **Mood:** curious
- **Characters:** Jack Harris
- **Duration:** 10s

Jack finds an old radio while cleaning the lighthouse.

### Scene 2: First Message
- **Setting:** lighthouse attic
- **Mood:** perplexed
- **Characters:** Jack Harris
- **Duration:** 12s

Jack turns on the radio and hears a faint distress call from a ship that vanished decades ago.

### Scene 3: Research
- **Setting:** lighthouse library
- **Mood:** intrigued
- **Characters:** Jack Harris
- **Duration:** 15s

Jack researches the history of the ship and its disappearance.

### Scene 4: Nighttime Transmission
- **Setting:** lighthouse living room at night
- **Mood:** haunting
- **Characters:** Jack Harris
- **Duration:** 20s

Jack listens to more messages from the radio, feeling an eerie connection to the past.

### Scene 5: Reflection
- **Setting:** lighthouse lantern room at sunset
- **Mood:** contemplative
- **Characters:** Jack Harris
- **Duration:** 30s

Jack reflects on his life and the significance of the messages, wondering if they're a metaphor for his own lost connections.

### Scene 6: The Final Message
- **Setting:** lighthouse attic
- **Mood:** bittersweet
- **Characters:** Jack Harris
- **Duration:** 13s

Jack hears a final, poignant message that leaves him with more questions than answers.

### Scene 7: The Waves Whisper On
- **Setting:** lighthouse window at dusk
- **Mood:** mysterious
- **Characters:** Jack Harris
- **Duration:** 10s

Jack stands at the lighthouse window, listening to the waves, as the radio falls silent in the background.
