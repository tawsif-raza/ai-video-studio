# The Last Tree on Earth

**Logline:** A centuries-old tree witnesses the destruction of its forest home and the eventual restoration by a group of determined children.

**Theme:** Hope and Resilience in the Face of Environmental Degradation  
**Tone:** cinematic, emotional, hopeful, documentary-style  
**Target Duration:** 150s

## Characters

- **Methuselah** (The Centuries-Old Tree): A wise and ancient tree that has seen the rise and fall of its forest home
- **Lily** (Leader of the Children): A determined and passionate young girl who leads the effort to restore the forest
- **Dr. Patel** (Forest Ecologist): A knowledgeable scientist who guides the children in their reforestation efforts
