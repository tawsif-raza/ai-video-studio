# Road Safety Lesson

**Logline:** A young fox learns to navigate a busy road safely with the help of a wise old owl.

**Theme:** Safety and Awareness  
**Tone:** Educational  
**Target Duration:** 20s

## Characters

- **Finnley the Fox** (Protagonist): A curious and adventurous young fox
- **Olwen the Owl** (Mentor): A wise and experienced owl who teaches road safety

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Introduction to Danger
- **Setting:** Roadside
- **Mood:** Caution
- **Characters:** Finnley the Fox
- **Duration:** 4s

Finnley approaches a busy road and hesitates.

### Scene 2: Meeting the Mentor
- **Setting:** Roadside
- **Mood:** Hopeful
- **Characters:** Finnley the Fox, Olwen the Owl
- **Duration:** 5s

Olwen the Owl appears and offers to teach Finnley about road safety.

### Scene 3: Safe Crossing
- **Setting:** Roadside
- **Mood:** Empowering
- **Characters:** Finnley the Fox, Olwen the Owl
- **Duration:** 6s

Olwen teaches Finnley how to safely cross the road.

### Scene 4: Successful Crossing
- **Setting:** Other side of the road
- **Mood:** Triumphant
- **Characters:** Finnley the Fox, Olwen the Owl
- **Duration:** 5s

Finnley successfully crosses the road with Olwen's guidance.
