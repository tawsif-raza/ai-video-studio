# Trial by Fire

**Logline:** A young chef must navigate a chaotic kitchen to prove their skills.

**Theme:** Perseverance  
**Tone:** Intense  
**Target Duration:** 30s

## Characters

- **Alex Chen** (Protagonist): A determined and talented young chef
- **Chef Thompson** (Mentor): A seasoned chef with high standards

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Introduction to Chaos
- **Setting:** Commercial Kitchen
- **Mood:** Overwhelming
- **Characters:** Alex Chen, Chef Thompson
- **Duration:** 5s

Alex enters a chaotic kitchen on their first day.

### Scene 2: The Challenge
- **Setting:** Commercial Kitchen
- **Mood:** Intense
- **Characters:** Alex Chen, Chef Thompson
- **Duration:** 8s

Chef Thompson assigns Alex a difficult dish to prepare.

### Scene 3: The Moment of Truth
- **Setting:** Commercial Kitchen
- **Mood:** Anxious
- **Characters:** Alex Chen, Chef Thompson
- **Duration:** 12s

Alex presents their dish to Chef Thompson for judgment.

### Scene 4: The Verdict
- **Setting:** Commercial Kitchen
- **Mood:** Relieved
- **Characters:** Alex Chen, Chef Thompson
- **Duration:** 5s

Chef Thompson provides feedback on Alex's dish.
